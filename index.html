THE ONLY SAFE NETWORK IS THE ONE NOTHING TOUCHES

You are a single program — one lantern of light in a dark network. Things come for the nodes tonight. Your tools each defend something real. Most things you face, the wrong tool simply will not stop. Find the right one before it finds a node.

The network, plainly: the glowing nodes are computers and services — a database, a mail server, the kind of thing a company dies without. The lines are the cables they talk over; packets — small bundles of data — constantly travel along them. You are a defense program, the only one left. Three kinds of attack come for a network: floods (junk traffic meant to drown services), malware (code that shouldn't be running), and spoofed packets (lies dressed as your own traffic). Every tool you carry answers exactly one of them.
